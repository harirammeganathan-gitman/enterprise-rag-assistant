# Example Company Handbook

This sample handbook is demonstration content only. Replace it with documents your
organization is authorized to process.

## Working hours and leave

The standard work week is Monday through Friday. Core collaboration hours are
10:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m. local time. Employees receive 20 days of paid annual leave
per calendar year. Leave requests should be submitted to a manager at least five
working days before the planned start date.

## Equipment and security

Employees may request one company laptop through the IT service desk. Lost or
stolen devices must be reported to IT and the employee's manager within 24 hours.
Company devices must use full-disk encryption and automatic screen locking.
Passwords must be unique, at least 14 characters long, and stored in the approved
company password manager.

## Expenses and travel

Business travel requires manager approval before booking. Meal expenses are
reimbursed up to 60 USD per day while traveling. Receipts are required for any
single expense above 25 USD. Expense reports should be submitted within 30 days
after the trip ends.

## Benefits and learning

The company provides an annual learning allowance of 1,200 USD per employee.
Course and certification requests require manager approval. Health benefit
enrollment begins on the first day of the month after an employee's start date.

## Support and incident reporting

The IT service desk is available Monday through Friday, 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m.
local time. Critical security incidents should be reported immediately using the
security hotline. Employees should not share confidential company information
with unapproved external services.
